About TapBot

TapBot is a student-developed awareness initiative created to address one of the most overlooked environmental problems-everyday water wastage.

The project focuses on encouraging responsible water habits through awareness, preventive thinking, and sustainability-oriented innovation. Instead of depending only on complex systems, TapBot demonstrates how small actions and simple technological ideas can collectively save thousands of litres of water.

TapBot highlights issues such as unattended taps, leakage, negligence in schools and public places, and unnecessary water usage habits that silently contribute to environmental stress.

The project represents a larger philosophy inside the Bot Ecosystem: using awareness-driven technology to solve real-world problems responsibly.
